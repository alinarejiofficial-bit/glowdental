Solutions for every case

Glow Dent delivers advanced dental care solutions focused on precision and quality. We ensure comfort, reliability, and lasting results in every treatment we provide.

Essix Retainer

Clear, custom-fit trays that hold teeth in place after orthodontic treatment.

Metal Crown

Alloy restorations that protect weak teeth and resist chewing forces.

Pilot surgical guides

Guides the initial drill for accurate implant position and inclination.

PFM Crown (Porcelain-Fused-to-Metal)

Metal strength with a tooth-colored porcelain finish.

PMMA Crown

High-quality temporary restorations for provisional and complex cases.

Stackable Guide

Modular multi-component guides for complex full-arch implant cases.

Zirconia Crown

Strong, metal-free ceramic caps with a natural, durable finish.

Dental Implant Crown

Precision-fitted caps designed for implant abutments and daily function.

Tooth supported surgical guides

Retained by teeth for single implants or partially edentulous cases.

Tissue supported surgical guides

Supported by mucosa, often based on an existing removable prosthesis.

Bone supported surgical guides

For edentulous patients, supported by load-bearing alveolar ridge areas.
